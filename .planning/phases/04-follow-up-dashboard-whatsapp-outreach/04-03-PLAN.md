| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-04-06 | Tampering | Anchor target=_blank para wa.me | mitigate | Sempre `rel="noopener noreferrer"` no anchor (evita tabnabbing via window.opener) |

| T-04-SC | Tampering | Supply chain (npm) | accept | Zero instalações neste plano (encodeURIComponent nativo; lucide-react já presente) |
</threat_model>
